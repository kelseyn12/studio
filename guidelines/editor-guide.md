# Editing with Studio

Studio is where Kelsey sends you videos and where you send them back. One page, one folder per
batch, one drop zone. Nothing else to learn.

Site: https://system-studio.fly.dev

## 1. Sign in

1. Kelsey invites you. You get an email from Studio.
2. Open it and sign up with **that same email**. Google sign-in works if it is the same address.
3. You land on **Cuts**. That is your page. Bookmark it.

## 2. What is on Cuts

One **folder per batch**, named by Kelsey (for example "Polsia Millionaire"). Each folder says
how many videos are inside and how many are finished.

Open a folder:

- **Download all** — saves every clean video to your computer in one click. If your browser
  asks to allow multiple downloads, say yes.
- **Mix 1, Mix 2, Mix 3 …** — each mix is one video. Under it:
  - **Hook** — the words on screen at the start.
  - **Body** — words for the middle, if any.
  - **CTA** — the words at the end.
  - A line that says what Kelsey needs back: usually **One file for every app**.
  - **Cutting** means it is yours to do. **To approve** means you already sent it.
- **Drop the finished videos here** — where your exports go.

## 3. Your job

Each clean video already has the cuts, music, and pacing. It has **no words on it**. You add:

1. The **Hook** words on screen at the start. Use the exact words shown. Kelsey tests hooks
   word for word, so one changed word is a different test.
2. **Body** and **CTA** words when they are listed.
3. **Captions** for everything spoken.
4. Anything that makes it feel native: a cut, a zoom, a pause. Do not change the order of the
   clips or re-cut the story.

Export: 1080 × 1920, mp4, under 250 MB, no watermark.

## 4. How many files per mix

**One.** The same file posts to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube. Name it with the mix
number:

| Mix | File name |
| --- | --- |
| Mix 1 | `mix 1.mp4` |
| Mix 2 | `mix 2.mp4` |
| Mix 12 | `mix 12.mp4` |

`mix 2`, `Mix 2`, `mix-2`, `mix_2`, `mix2` all work. The number must match the mix.

### The rare case: a mix says "Two files"

Once in a while a mix will say **Two files: IG · FB + TT · YT**. That means Kelsey wants the
text styled two ways — one export that looks native on Instagram, one that looks native on
TikTok. Same video, same words, two text styles. Add the look to the name:

| Which | File name |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram style | `mix 2 IG.mp4` |
| TikTok style | `mix 2 TT.mp4` |

If a mix does not say "Two files", ignore this section.

## 5. Send them back

1. Open the folder on Cuts.
2. Drag your exports onto **Drop the finished videos here** (or click it and pick them). Up to
   12 at a time.
3. Wait for "N files in". Each file lands on its mix, a ✓ appears, and the mix moves to
   **To approve**. Kelsey gets a ping.

You do not schedule, pick a thumbnail, or write the caption. Kelsey does that.

## 6. If something goes wrong

- **"Name the file with its mix number"** — the name has no `mix N`. Rename it and drop again.
- **"Over the size limit"** — export smaller. 250 MB max.
- **It landed on the wrong mix** — tell Kelsey. She removes it, you drop it again with the
  right name.
- **Kelsey wants a change** — the mix comes back as **Cutting** with a note. Fix it, export,
  drop it with the same name. The newest file is the one that posts.
- **Words look wrong or are missing** — ask before guessing. Hook / Body / CTA in the folder
  are the source of truth.

## 7. One-off videos

A video that is not from a batch opens on its own page. There you get:

- **Your job** — Kelsey's note, a CapCut link if there is one, and the words to put on.
- **Download files** — the clean video, clips, voice note, references.
- **Which video is this?** — leave it on **Both** and drop one file. Pick IG · FB or TT · YT
  only if Kelsey asked for two.
- Or paste a direct mp4 link instead of dropping.

---

## Kelsey's side (not for the editor)

1. **Multiply** → Mix settings → turn **Draw the words on the video** off. One clean file per
   mix. Hook / Body / CTA are saved on each video for him.
2. Leave **Text look** on Match the accounts. Only set it to **Both looks** when you truly
   want two text styles; that is what makes a mix say "Two files" in his folder.
3. Generate. Spot-check a couple.
4. **Send all to editor to polish** on the batch page. Add a note if there is something to keep
   or avoid. The whole batch lands on Cuts as one folder.
5. He drops files back. Each video shows **To approve** on Cuts and Today.
6. Open the video → Live: watch, pick the thumbnail, check accounts, caption (blank stays
   blank), schedule. Needs a fix? Send it back to the editor with a note.
7. Add him once on **Team** with his email → Invite. Mark him **default** so Send never asks.

Fly cost does not change for this. Multiply still renders on your Mac.
