# Editing with Studio

Studio is where Kelsey sends you videos and where you send them back. One page, one drop zone.
Nothing else to learn.

Site: https://system-studio.fly.dev

## 1. Sign in

1. Kelsey invites you. You get an email from Studio.
2. Open it and sign up with **that same email**. Google sign-in works if it is the same address.
3. You land on **Cuts**. That is your page. Bookmark it.
4. When a job is sent, Studio pings you in the **Studio- Editing** Discord. You do not have to keep the page open.

## 2. Two kinds of video

Kelsey makes two kinds of content, and they come to you two different ways.

| | Batch videos | Full edits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What they are | Screen-recording / tech videos made in bulk ("Canvas / tech" deals) | Traditional UGC: Kelsey on camera, talking |
| What you get | A clean, already-cut video with **no words on it** | Raw clips, a script, a note, often a reference video |
| What you do | Put the words on, add captions, make it feel native | Cut the whole video from scratch |
| Where it shows | A **folder** on Cuts, named by batch | A single card under **To cut** on Cuts |
| Deadline | Usually a few days; Kelsey will say | Shown on the card as **Due …** |

Both end the same way: you drop a finished mp4, it goes to Kelsey for approval.

## 3. Batch videos — put the words on

### What you see

One **folder per batch** on Cuts, named by Kelsey (for example "Polsia Millionaire"). It says how
many videos are inside and how many are finished.

Open the folder:

- **Download all** — saves every clean video in one click. If your browser asks to allow multiple
  downloads, say yes.
- **Mix 1, Mix 2, Mix 3 …** — each mix is one video. Under it:
  - **Hook** — the words on screen at the start.
  - **Body** — words for the middle, if any.
  - **CTA** — the words at the end.
  - A line saying what Kelsey needs back, usually **One file for every app**.
  - **Cutting** = yours to do. **To approve** = already sent.
- **Drop the finished videos here** — where your exports go.

### What you do

The clean video already has the cuts, music, and pacing. You add:

1. The **Hook** words on screen at the start. Exact words. Kelsey tests hooks word for word, so
   one changed word is a different test.
2. **Body** and **CTA** words when they are listed.
3. **Captions** for everything spoken.
4. Anything that makes it feel native: a cut, a zoom, a pause. Do not reorder the clips or re-cut
   the story.

### Name the files

**One file per mix.** It posts to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube as is.

| Mix | File name |
| --- | --- |
| Mix 1 | `mix 1.mp4` |
| Mix 2 | `mix 2.mp4` |
| Mix 12 | `mix 12.mp4` |

`mix 2`, `Mix 2`, `mix-2`, `mix_2`, `mix2` all work. The number must match the mix.

**Rare case:** a mix says **Two files: IG · FB + TT · YT**. That means Kelsey wants the text
styled two ways: one export that looks native on Instagram, one that looks native on TikTok. Same
video, same words, two text styles. Add the look to the name: `mix 2 IG.mp4` and `mix 2 TT.mp4`.
If a mix does not say "Two files", ignore this.

### Send them back

1. Open the folder on Cuts.
2. Drag your exports onto **Drop the finished videos here** (or click it and pick them). Up to 12
   at a time.
3. Wait for "N files in". Each lands on its mix, a ✓ appears, and the mix moves to **To approve**.

## 4. Full edits — cut it from the clips

### What you see

A card under **To cut** on Cuts with the video's name, the deal, and **Due …** if there is a
deadline. Click it to open the job. It stays in **To cut** until you press **Start cutting**.
Watching, downloading, or opening Drive does not move it. On the job page:

- **Your job** — the deadline, Kelsey's note, the **words to put on the video** (hook / CTA if
  any), the **script** (what is said; open it to read), and **reference** links when she
  wants you to match a feel. Each link has a note saying what to copy.
- A checklist of what is in the packet: hook, script, clips, note.
- The **voice note** at the top. Listen before you read.
- **Your CapCut link** — paste your project so she can open it. She does not send you hers. If CapCut asks her to join, invite her email on that project.
- **Download files** — raw clips, reference files, and **Open the Google Drive folder**.
- **Which video is this?** — leave it on **One video**. IG · FB and TT · YT are only when she asked for two exports.
- The drop zone, or a Google Drive link. Press Attach URL. The link can be the video, or a folder with the video in it. Her folder or one you made. Make the Drive link public before you paste it.

### What you do

1. Listen to the voice note. Read the words and the script.
2. Open Google Drive for the raw footage. Press **Start cutting** when you begin.
3. Cut the video to the script: hook up top, tight pacing, captions on everything spoken, hook
   words on screen if they are listed. Match each reference, and follow the note under it.
4. Export 1080 × 1920 mp4, under 250 MB, no watermark. Any file name is fine here.
5. Paste a Drive link to the finished video and press Attach URL. Make it public first. It can be in her folder, or a folder you made. Dropping the mp4 on the job still works. Studio pings her.

### Deadlines

**Due …** on the card is the day Kelsey needs it in Studio, not the day it posts. If you are going
to miss it, tell her before the day, not after.

## 5. Revisions — both kinds

Kelsey watches it on her side. Two things can happen:

- **Approved.** Discord pings you. She can add a short note, like "good job". You are done.
- **Needs a change.** Discord pings you once. The video moves to the top of Cuts, under **Needs changes**, and a gold bar stays at the top of the app until you drop the new video. Open the job: the title is **Needs changes**, and her fix is the first thing on the page. A screenshot of a font, text style, or frame sits with that fix. The voice note, words, script, and references are under **Original brief** if you need them. Fix it, export, and drop the new video the same way (same file name for batch videos). The newest file is the one that posts. If you miss Discord, the bar and the Cuts list are still there. If the fix is still open after 4 hours, Discord pings you again, then once a day until the new video is in.

You never schedule, pick thumbnails, or write captions.

## 6. If something goes wrong

- **"Name the file with its mix number"** — a batch drop with no `mix N` in the name. Rename it
  and drop again.
- **"Over the size limit"** — export smaller. 250 MB max.
- **It landed on the wrong mix** — tell Kelsey. She removes it, you drop it again with the right
  name.
- **Words look wrong or are missing** — ask before guessing. Hook / Body / CTA and the script in
  Studio are the source of truth.
- **No files on a full edit** — the checklist will show what is missing. Tell Kelsey.

## Kelsey's side (not for the editor)

**Batch videos (Canvas / tech)**

1. **Multiply** → Mix settings → turn **Draw the words on the video** off. One clean file per mix.
   Hook / Body / CTA are saved on each video for him.
2. Leave **Text look** on Match the accounts. Only pick **Both looks** when you want two text
   styles; that is what makes a mix say "Two files" in his folder.
3. Generate, spot-check, then **Send all to editor to polish** on the batch page. Add a note if
   there is something to keep or avoid. The batch lands on Cuts as one folder.

**Full edits (Traditional UGC)**

1. Open the video → **Write**: hook, script, reference links (a note under each one), **editor deadline**. These all show on
   his job.
2. **Clips**: drop raw clips or paste the 4K Drive folder, add a voice note or written note, a
   reference clip. **Send to editor**. It lands under his To cut. It moves to Cutting when he presses Start cutting.

**Both**

3. He drops files back. Each video shows **To approve** on Cuts and Today.
4. Open the video → Live: watch, pick the thumbnail, check accounts, caption (blank stays blank),
   schedule. Needs a fix? Send it back to the editor with a note.
5. Add him once on **Team** with his email → Invite. Mark him **default** so Send never asks.
6. Discord pings only when work changes hands. You send him a job or a polish, or he drops the finished cut. Your own drop, Schedule, and a note stay quiet. Keys live in `.env` (`DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN`, `DISCORD_CHANNEL_ID`, `DISCORD_EDITOR_ID`, `DISCORD_CREATOR_ID`). The same four have to be on Fly or a drop on the public site stays quiet.

Fly cost does not change for any of this. Multiply still renders on your Mac.
