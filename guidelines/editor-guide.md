# Editing with Studio — guide for the editor

Studio is where Kelsey hands you videos and where you hand them back. You never need anything
else in it. One page, one folder per batch, one drop zone.

Site: https://system-studio.fly.dev

## 1. Getting in

1. Kelsey invites you from her Team page. You get an email from Studio (Clerk).
2. Open the email and sign up with **that same email address**. Google sign-in works if the
   email matches.
3. You land on **Cuts**. That is your only page. Bookmark it.

If you ever see a blank Cuts page with "No batch folders yet", nothing has been sent to you.
Check back or ask Kelsey.

## 2. What you see on Cuts

**Batches** — one folder per Multiply batch, named the way Kelsey named it, like
"Polsia Millionaire". Each folder shows how many videos are in it and how many are finished.

Open a folder and you get:

- **Download all N clean videos** — saves every video in the batch to your computer in one
  click. Your browser may ask once to allow multiple downloads. Say yes.
- A list of **mixes**: Mix 1, Mix 2, Mix 3 … Each mix is one video. Under each mix:
  - **Hook** — the words that go on screen at the start.
  - **Body** — words for the middle, if any.
  - **CTA** — the words at the end.
  - **Needs** — which finished files Kelsey needs back for this mix. See section 4.
  - A stage: *Cutting* (yours to do) or *To approve* (you already sent it back).
- **Drop the finished videos here** — the drop zone for the whole folder.

Below the folders there may be loose videos under **To cut**. Those are one-offs. Click one to
open it; it works the same way but on a single page.

## 3. Your job on each video

The clean video already has the cuts, the music, the pacing. It has **no words on it**. You add:

1. **The hook text** on screen at the start — the exact words in **Hook**. Keep the words as
   written. Kelsey tests hooks word for word, so a changed word is a different test.
2. **Body / CTA text** when those lines are listed.
3. **Captions** for everything spoken, in a clean native style.
4. Anything that makes it feel native to the app: a cut, a zoom, a pause. Do not re-cut the
   story or change the order of the clips.

Export at 1080 × 1920, mp4, under 250 MB. No watermark.

## 4. One file or two?

Instagram and TikTok draw text differently, so when a deal posts to both, Kelsey needs two
exports of the same video:

- **IG · FB** — Instagram style text. Posts to Instagram and Facebook.
- **TT · YT** — TikTok style text. Posts to TikTok and YouTube.

Under each mix, **Needs** tells you which:

- `Needs IG · FB + TT · YT — two files, one for each` → export twice, one text style each.
- `Needs Every app` → one export, it goes everywhere.

A ✓ appears next to a look once that file is in.

## 5. Naming the files — this is the important part

The drop zone sorts files by their **name**. Name every export with the mix number and the look:

| What it is | Name it |
| --- | --- |
| Mix 2, Instagram look | `mix 2 IG.mp4` |
| Mix 2, TikTok look | `mix 2 TT.mp4` |
| Mix 2, one file for every app | `mix 2.mp4` |
| Mix 12, Instagram look | `mix 12 IG.mp4` |

Rules:

- Always include `mix` and the number. `mix 2`, `mix-2`, `mix_2`, `Mix2` all work.
- `IG`, `FB`, `insta`, `instagram` all mean the Instagram file. `TT`, `YT`, `tiktok`,
  `youtube`, `shorts` all mean the TikTok file.
- Nothing after the number means every app.
- A file without a mix number is refused with a message. Rename it and drop again.
- The number must match the mix in the folder. `mix 12` never lands on mix 1.

## 6. Sending them back

1. Open the folder on Cuts.
2. Drag all your exports onto **Drop the finished videos here**, or click it and pick them.
   Up to 12 at a time.
3. Wait for "N files in". Each one lands on its mix, the ✓ appears, and the mix moves to
   *To approve*. Kelsey gets a ping.

That is it. You do not schedule, pick a thumbnail, or write the caption. Kelsey does that on
her side.

## 7. If something is wrong

- **"Name the file with its mix number"** — the file name has no `mix N`. Rename it.
- **A file is over the size limit** — export smaller. 250 MB max.
- **Wrong mix got the file** — tell Kelsey. She can delete the file from that video and you
  drop it again with the right name.
- **A video needs a fix after she looks at it** — it comes back to your folder as *Cutting*
  with a note. Fix it, export, drop it with the same name. The newest file is the one that posts.
- **Words look wrong or are missing** — ask Kelsey before guessing. The Hook / Body / CTA on
  the folder are the source of truth.

## 8. Single-video jobs

A video that is not from a Multiply batch opens on its own page. There you see:

- **Your job** — Kelsey's note, the CapCut link if there is one, and "Words to put on the video".
- **Download files** — the clips, the voice note, references, and the clean video.
- **Which video is this?** — pick IG · FB, TT · YT, or Both, then drop one file.
- Or paste a direct mp4 link instead of dropping.

## Kelsey's side (not for the editor)

1. **Multiply** → Mix settings → turn **Draw the words on the video** off. One clean file per
   mix. Hook / Body / CTA are saved on each video for him.
2. Generate. Check a couple of outputs.
3. **Send all to editor to polish** on the batch page. Add a note if there is something to keep
   or avoid. The whole batch moves to Cuts as one folder under the batch name.
4. He drops files back. Each video shows **To approve** on Cuts and Today.
5. Open the video → Live step: watch, pick the thumbnail, check the accounts, write the caption
   (blank stays blank), schedule. If it needs a fix, send it back to the editor with a note.
6. Add him once on **Team** with his email → Invite. Mark him **default** so Send never asks.

Fly cost does not change for this flow. Multiply still renders on your Mac; Fly only serves
pages and files.
